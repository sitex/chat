# Structure

- `chatcore/`: reusable package modules and deployment templates.
- `tests/`: package, roundtable, single-instance, and TTS-helper tests.
- `scripts/`: fleet checks, avatar generation, and roundtable utilities.
- `docs/`: consumer guidance and historical design/plan documents.
- `.planning/`: GSD state; `.continue-here.md` and `HANDOFF.json` are preserved legacy evidence.
